# सम्पाती और जटायु — Storyboard v2 (realistic cinematic)

**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, ~2:45
**Look:** realistic, cinematic Indian-mythology concept art (painterly realism, film lighting, volumetric light, mist, depth of field). No cartoon shapes.
**Method:** drawn entirely in code (no AI images), animated with camera moves, light rays, embers, mist, feathers, flashes and cross-dissolves.
**Audio:** narration — *Viraj – Calm Devotional Narrator* (ElevenLabs v4). During dialogue the narrator is silent.
Characters — Ravana: *Ayush M (deep)* · old Jatayu: *Grandpa Danish* · Sita: *Nitya* · Rama: *Pranit* · Sampati: *Rohit* · Angad: *Yugandhar* · young Jatayu/Sampati: young voices.
Music: tanpura/flute drone, soft drums only in the fight. Real-sounding SFX.

### Character design notes (for the reference sheets)
- **Jatayu (old):** huge Himalayan vulture, silver-grey and white feathers, pale pink bald head, wise eyes, a thin gold ornament at the neck.
- **Jatayu / Sampati (young):** glossy dark-brown feathers; Sampati larger, chestnut-brown, cream ruff.
- **Sampati (old):** wingless/burnt stubs, dusty brown, deep-set eyes, cream ruff.
- **Ravana:** ten heads, black-gold armour, tall gold crown, curled moustache, purple sash; menacing.
- **Sita:** red-and-gold saree, gold jewellery, long braid, gentle face.
- **Rama:** blue-tinted skin, saffron/yellow dhoti, matted hair top-knot (vanvas), bow and quiver; **Lakshmana:** fair, similar forest attire.
- **Hanuman / Angad / Jambavan:** realistic vanara warriors (Hanuman with mace), Jambavan an elder bear-warrior.
- **Sone ki Lanka:** golden palaces and gopurams on three-peaked Trikuta, walls, lamps, reflection in the sea.
